🌌 Lets Add Just More
A 1.20.1 Forge Experience | Version 1.0.0

Lets Add Just More is a cosmic expansion that introduces gravity manipulation, high-tier stealth gear, and a haunting new dimension. Harness the power of Eclipsium to become a master of the Void.

🧭 Progression & Features
Eclipsium Orbs: Rare resources found in Overworld Deepslate veins. Used to craft Eclipsium Blocks and high-tier gear.

The Igniter: A heavy-duty tool crafted with a Flint and Steel surrounded by 8 Diamonds.

The Portal: Build a frame of Eclipsium Blocks and use the Igniter to enter the Eclipsium Dimension.

Voidcore: The ultimate endgame ore, found only in the deepest reaches of the Eclipsium dimension.

🛡️ The Eclipsium "Ghost" Set
Helmet: Eclipsium Sight (Night Vision).

Chestplate: Gravity Well (Slows mobs) & Ghost Phase (Invis/Invincibility while sneaking).

Leggings: Shadow Dash (Speed V while sneaking).

Boots: Gravity Dampeners (No fall damage).

🔌 Compatibility & Optional Configs
We believe in "Adding Just More" to your existing modpacks. The download includes an Optional folder containing:

🌱 Mystical Agriculture Integration: Custom configurations to add Eclipsium Seeds and Voidcore Seeds to your farm.

📜 KubeJS Scripts: Pre-written scripts to easily modify Eclipsium recipes, loot tables, and dimension properties for your specific modpack needs.

📂 How to use the Optional Folder:
Open the .zip file.

Drag the config and kubejs folders into your Minecraft instance folder.

Restart the game to see the new crops and custom scripts!
